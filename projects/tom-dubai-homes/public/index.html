Where to search

  • Bayut has TruCheck verified listings and transaction history.
  • Property Finder has the most villa stock and agent ratings.
  • Dubizzle sometimes has direct-from-landlord rentals with no agency fee.
  • DLD Rental Index shows how much a landlord can legally raise the rent at renewal.

Renting checklist

  • Ask for fewer cheques. Landlords often drop the price 3–5% for 1 or 2 cheques.
  • Check the Ejari can be registered and the title deed matches the landlord.
  • Agree who pays for maintenance and get it in the tenancy contract.
  • Photo every room at move-in to protect the 5% deposit.

Buying checklist

  • Get mortgage pre-approval before viewing. Expat first homes need at least 20% down (30% above AED 5M).
  • Ask for the service charge from the DLD Mollak index, not the agent's estimate.
  • Check recent sales on the same street in DLD transaction data.
  • Book a snagging inspection before transfer. It costs about AED 2–4k.

Should Tom rent or buy?

Compares the total cost of owning against renting the same home over time. Buying costs include fees, mortgage interest, service charges and upkeep, minus the equity he builds. Renting costs include rent, plus the investment return he gives up by tying cash into a deposit.

Upkeep is set at 1% of the price per year. Selling costs are 2% agency. Use "Rent vs buy" on any listing card to load its numbers here.
