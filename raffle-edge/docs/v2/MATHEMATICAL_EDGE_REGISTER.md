## E05: Free postal entry on expensive tickets has positive EV (ex-ante rule)

- **Platform:** 7days Performance
- **Status:** SUPPORTED
- **Mathematical explanation:** A postcard earns t = ceil(91p/price) tickets (min 1) with equal odds. Floor EV/card = t*V/(max+t). Rule: post only if floor EV > cost/card (91p stamp + 15p card = 106p).
- **Data requirements:** 200 sampled competitions incl. tickets_per_postcard; 7days T&Cs 3.5.7 (A)
- **Historical results:** 16/200 (8.4%, Bayes 95% CrI 5.0-12.6%) qualify; train period 7/103 selected, all realised EV > cost
- **Validation results:** Test period (Sep-Nov) 9/97 selected; realised EV/cost mean 2.98 (bootstrap 95% CI 1.71-5.38); 100% above cost
- **Statistical significance:** Floor EV > cost by construction for selected competitions; realised EV confirmed out of sample
- **Confidence interval:** EV/cost 1.71-5.38
- **Practical accessibility:** Legal, terms-permitted route (multiple postcards allowed up to the per-person limit, each posted separately; account required)
- **Estimated advantage:** Mean ~GBP 3.16 expected value per GBP 1.06 card on qualifying competitions
- **Costs:** 91p stamp + postcard + ~3 min per card; P(win) per card ~0.16%: ~442 cards (~GBP 470) for a 50% chance of one win
- **Legal considerations:** Statutory free-entry route (Gambling Act 2005 Sch 2 para 8). Personal use only: selling it as a 'way to win' risks DMCC Act 2024 Sch 20 para 18.
