# GOV.UK Voluntary Code of Good Practice for Prize Draw Operators: summary

- Collection page: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/voluntary-code-of-good-practice-for-prize-draw-operators (snapshot sha256 0a8ec7c347ab5f41237a7f4ddfee000cc9b71c301e76d259320528e67dadd407)
- Code text (HTML): https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/voluntary-code-of-good-practice-for-prize-draw-operators/voluntary-code-of-good-practice-for-prize-draw-operators (snapshot sha256 87170358e1831459e6cedeac610ba7942c88e480781c5bd230011e3c21007410)
- Published 20 November 2025, last updated 19 October 2026, accessed 2026-11-23. It is voluntary and DCMS has oversight: "This Code is not legislation, and does not replace existing consumer or advertising laws."
- Implementation date: "no later than 20 May 2026". Operators who sign after that date must comply from the start.

## Draw fairness and independence (clause 2.2)
> "This should be by an independent person, or under the supervision of an independent person, unless winners are selected by a computer process that produces verifiably random and auditable results, or by a certified physical drawing machine."

> "Entries to any variant of prize draw product offered by an operator (whether via the free entry route or the paid entry route) should have an equal chance of winning each available prize. Physical drawing devices which are certified should not require supervision. Operators should provide details of the mechanism used for draws on their website."

Clause 2.1 says each product needs a clear summary of how the draw is run, and it must include "a statement that the prizes are to be awarded in accordance with the laws of chance."

## Information on odds (clause 2.3)
> "Where possible, prior to entering a draw, operators should provide players with clear and easily accessible information regarding the likelihood of winning a prize and how prizes will be allocated. Whilst the exact likelihood of winning a prize will depend on the number of tickets sold, information of that nature could include, but is not limited to, the maximum number of tickets available (where applicable) or data from previous comparable prize draws."

## Free entry (clause 2.4, plus 1.10 for instant wins)
> "Operators should clearly and prominently provide players with details of any “free entry” option(s) for the prize draw before the point of purchase, and in line with the requirements of the Act."

Free entry can be by ordinary post, or by any other method that is "neither more expensive nor less convenient than entering the draw by a paid entry route". Operators must allow enough time for free entries to arrive. An ineffective free route "is very likely to signify that persons are, in practice, required to pay to participate". Clause 1.10 says instant wins need the same equivalence between paid and free routes, and "Instant win prize draws should not form the majority of an operator’s total number of competitions at any given time."

## Draw integrity and low sales (clause 2.5)
> "To ensure the integrity of each draw, operators should not provide a prize of lower value due to low ticket sales, change the date on which a draw ends, or cancel a draw due to low ticket sales."

## Publishing winners
The Code has **no** clause that requires operators to publish winners or draw results. The nearest requirements are the mechanism disclosure in 2.2 and clause 3.4: "Operators should publish all of the measures they have in place with regards to player protections, transparency and accountability, ensuring their adherence with this Code is transparently displayed on their websites."

## Other key player protections (section 1)
- 18+ only, with reasonable age verification (1.1).
- Credit cards: "Operators should not accept credit card payments in excess of £250 per month per player" and none at all for instant-win draws (1.3).
- Monthly spend limits, which players must be able to set to £0 (1.4).
- Account suspension for at least 6 months (1.5).
- Harm monitoring and intervention (1.6–1.8).
- An appropriate interval between a draw opening and closing (1.9).
- CAP/BCAP-compliant, socially responsible advertising (1.11).

## Accountability (section 3)
- Operators monitor their own compliance (3.1).
- Third parties such as affiliates and draw-management partners must follow the Code (3.2).
- Operators publish the measures they have in place (3.4).
- Complaints about non-compliance go to prizedrawcode@dcms.gov.uk.
