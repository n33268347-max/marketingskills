## Safeguarding policy

Accrington Lions Community Boxing Club C.I.C. is [an affiliated member of / applying to affiliate to] England Boxing. As such, it adopts the following Club Child Protection Policy, which is supported by and complements the England Boxing National Child Protection Policy. These policies are mandatory, and accepting them is a non-negotiable requirement of becoming a member of this club. Copies of the England Boxing Child Protection Policy are available from the England Boxing office on 0114 223 5654 or from www.englandboxing.org.

- This club has a duty of care to children and young people (under 18 years of age), and their safety and welfare is paramount.
- This club acknowledges that it has a legal and moral responsibility to ensure the wellbeing of children and young people within the club.
- This club acknowledges that every child and young person has a right to enjoy membership and the sport of amateur boxing within a safe, enjoyable setting.
- This club will continually review this policy in the light of identified best practice.
- This club acknowledges that every member, including committee members, coaches, officials and volunteers, has a moral and legal responsibility to safeguard and promote every child and young person's welfare.
- This club is committed to ensuring that every child and young person, regardless of age, ability, racial origin, religious belief, sexual orientation or gender, has a right to learn, participate in and enjoy amateur boxing, free of fear and safe from abuse of any type.
- This club ensures that required members of the committee, coaches, officials and volunteers hold a current Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS) check at all times.
- The club has an appointed Club Welfare Officer, named below, who will:
  - give you an induction, including the England Boxing Child Protection Policy and instruction on child protection within the club and England Boxing, so that you understand the need for strict compliance;
  - administer this Child Safeguarding Policy;
  - ensure that confidential, detailed and accurate records of all safeguarding concerns are kept and securely stored.
- This club will treat seriously and deal rigorously with any incidence of abuse, and will at all times follow the principles and procedures in the England Boxing Child Safeguarding Policy, the Safeguarding Procedure and the England Boxing National Child Protection Policy.
- Any abuse or inappropriate behaviour towards children and young people will be challenged and dealt with appropriately.
- This club understands that abuse comes in many forms, including emotional, physical and sexual abuse. Foul language, bullying and requiring an athlete to train at an inappropriate level are also recognised as forms of abuse.
- The club acknowledges that it is the responsibility of experts to determine whether or not abuse is taking place or has taken place. It is, however, everyone's responsibility to report concerns or abuse.
- This club is committed to preventing the employment or deployment of unsuitable individuals.
- If a concern involves the Club Welfare Officer, or a member of their family, report it to the Deputy Club Welfare Officer or to England Boxing's Regional Safeguarding Officer (details below).

**We ALWAYS remember: in cases of concern or abuse, DOING NOTHING IS NOT AN OPTION.**

## England Boxing Child and Young People's Charter

England Boxing aims to ensure that all children and young people within the sport of boxing are treated with respect, valued, and kept safe from all forms of abuse at all times.

We want you:

- to feel welcomed into a safe, caring sport with a happy and friendly atmosphere within your club;
- to be regarded as an individual, listened to, and have your feelings respected and opinions valued;
- to be cared for by skilled, experienced staff and volunteers who understand the needs of children and young people;
- to be given praise and encouragement and opportunities to succeed, in an environment where positive behaviour is promoted;
- to be encouraged to develop self-discipline and consideration for others;
- to be given opportunities to learn new skills, develop confidence and a positive self-image.

## Monitoring

This policy will be reviewed **every year**, and also when:

- legislation or government guidance changes;
- required by the Local Safeguarding Children Partnership, UK Sport, Sport England or England Boxing's National Compliance Manager;
- any other significant change or event happens, including changes to England Boxing's Child Safeguarding Policy.

## Equity policy statement

Accrington Lions Community Boxing Club C.I.C. adopts the England Boxing Equity Policy and is committed to ensuring that equity is incorporated across all aspects of its development. In doing so it acknowledges and adopts the following Sport England definition of sports equity:

- Sports equity is about fairness in sport, equality of access, recognising inequalities and taking steps to ensure sport becomes equally accessible to everyone in society.
- The club respects the rights, dignity and worth of every person and will treat everyone equally within the context of their sport, regardless of age, ability, gender, race, ethnicity, religious belief, sexuality and social/economic status.
- The club is committed to everyone having the right to enjoy their sport in an environment free from threat of intimidation, harassment and abuse.
- All club members have a responsibility to oppose discriminatory behaviour and promote equality of opportunity.
- The club will deal with any incidence of discriminatory behaviour seriously, according to the club and England Boxing disciplinary procedures.

This policy is reviewed annually.
