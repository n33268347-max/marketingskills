## Q1. Community-by-community comparison: rents, prices, AED/sqft, sizes, yields, trends

### Takeaway
The cheapest space for 3-4 beds is at the outer belt:
- **DAMAC Hills 2:** about AED 870-1,100/sqft to buy; 3-bed rents about AED 93-112k.
- **Villanova / The Valley / Town Square:** about AED 1,450/sqft; 3-bed rents about AED 135-155k.
- **Mudon, Dubai South and Mirdif:** next cheapest tier.

The established "prime family" communities cost roughly two to three times as much per sqft and per bedroom:
- **Dubai Hills Estate:** about AED 2,400-2,900/sqft.
- **The Springs, Tilal Al Ghaf, Sobha Hartland:** about AED 2,000-2,500/sqft.
- **Jumeirah Golf Estates:** about AED 2,300-3,300/sqft.

Asking rents eased in late 2026, but sale prices held roughly flat.

- **Implied gross yields (inference):**
  - The Springs 3-bed: ~4.0-4.3% (rent ~236k / price 5.5-5.9M), below the 5-6% for outer-belt villas.
  - Villanova 3-bed: ~5.3% (152k / 2.87M).
- **Trend summary.** Late 2026 is a tenant-friendlier rental market than 2024-25. Asking rents are falling and listings have surged, especially for apartments and newer villa supply (AR3, Dubai South). Sale prices are flat to slightly up, with luxury and established communities (Springs, Sobha Hartland, Dubai Hills) the firmest. Renewing tenants are still seeing increases, so new-lease negotiation is where the leverage sits.

## Q2. Which communities give the most space per dirham for 3-4 beds, to rent and to buy?

### Takeaway
To **buy**, the most space per dirham is at:
- **DAMAC Hills 2:** about AED 870-1,100/sqft.
- **Dubai South / Emaar South:** about AED 1,360-1,390/sqft.
- **Villanova / The Valley:** about AED 1,450/sqft.
- **DAMAC Lagoons:** about AED 1,500/sqft.

Next come Al Furjan (about 1,680/sqft), Arabian Ranches 1-2 (about 1,430-1,680/sqft, with older and larger plots) and DAMAC Hills (about 1,900/sqft).

To **rent**, the cheapest 3-beds are in DAMAC Hills 2 (about AED 93-112k), followed by Town Square, The Valley and Villanova (about AED 135-155k), then Mudon (about AED 181k). Mirdif shows the lowest average villa rents among the established inner-city areas.

### Inferences
- **Rent example (inference).** A 3-bed townhouse in DAMAC Hills 2, The Valley or Town Square costs roughly 40-50% of a comparable Dubai Hills Estate rental, and roughly 55-65% of a Springs rental. The trade-off is longer commutes, often 30-45 min to DIFC/Marina, and smaller or younger communities.
- **Older-stock value.** Arabian Ranches 1/2 and The Springs/Meadows offer larger plots and established schools and amenities, but at materially higher entry prices than the new outer belt. Arabian Ranches is about 1,430-1,680/sqft against Dubai Hills at about 2,400-2,900/sqft, so it gives more space per dirham than Dubai Hills among established communities.
- **3-bed apartments vs townhouses.** A 3-bed apartment in Marina (~210k registered) or Downtown (~280k) rents for more than a 3-bed townhouse in Villanova, Town Square or Mudon. Townhouses in mid-tier communities therefore give substantially more space per dirham than prime apartments.

### Gaps
- No reliable AED per sqft of rent (rent ÷ built-up area) by community was found. The ranking above is by absolute rent and sale AED/sqft, not normalised rent per sqft.
- Mirdif and Al Barsha/Umm Suqeim villas, often cited for big plots at moderate prices, lack 2026 bedroom-level data.

## Q3. Where are service charges high or low, and how much do they add per year?

### Takeaway
Villa communities typically run about AED 2.5-5/sqft/yr; Arabian Ranches 2 at about AED 2.4 and Arabian Ranches 1 at about AED 3.1 are among the lowest. Apartment towers run much higher: about AED 15-30/sqft, and AED 25-50 on the Palm.

For a family home this means:
- **Villa or townhouse:** roughly AED 6-15k a year.
- **3-bed apartment:** roughly AED 30-60k+ a year.

Sources disagree, and I could not access the official DLD Mollak index directly.

### Cited Findings
- Arabian Ranches 1 averages ~AED 3.09/sqft and Arabian Ranches 2 ~AED 2.44/sqft. Arabian Ranches overall is ~AED 3-4/sqft, with some premium sub-communities as low as AED 1-2 (citing Christie's, Apr 2026). Outlier guides give 5-8 and even 12-16, which look unreliable. — [Luxhabitat service charge guide](https://www.luxhabitat.ae/the-journal/dubai-service-charges-guide/); [FC Real Estate Arabian Ranches](https://fcrealestate.ae/arabian-ranches-amenities-what-you-get-vs-what-you-pay/); [Excel Properties](https://excelproperties.ae/blog/property-and-community-service-charges-in-dubai)
- Established Emaar communities (Arabian Ranches, Dubai Hills Estate) carry RERA-approved rates typically between AED 2.50 and 5/sqft. — [search summary of agency guides](https://disruptiveestate.com/blog/dubai-property-service-charges-benchmark)
- Dubai Hills Estate: a DLD-based building index shows a median of ~AED 18.53/sqft across 26 buildings, range 2.92-29.94, which is mostly apartment towers. A 2026 Emaar breakdown lists Dubai Hills villas at ~AED 3/sqft. — [Continental Club service charges](https://continentalclub.ae/service-charges-dubai/); [Oliva by area](https://joinoliva.com/en/learn/blog/dubai-service-charges-by-area-and-developer-2026)
- DAMAC Hills: DLD-based median ~AED 3.13/sqft across 48 buildings, range 2.11-25.61. Another guide puts DAMAC Hills villas at AED 5-10, noting that golf course maintenance adds cost. — [Continental Club](https://continentalclub.ae/service-charges-dubai/); [Pearlshire](https://www.pearlshire.com/blogs/dubai-service-charges-2026)
- Jumeirah Golf Estates: AED 12-18/sqft/yr in one guide, attributed to golf and club infrastructure. This is uncorroborated. — [dubaifastliving](https://dubaifastliving.com/dubai-service-charges/)
- Palm Jumeirah: apartments AED 25-50/sqft and villas AED 8-45/sqft across sources. Lower estimates are 15-25 for both. — [Excel Properties](https://excelproperties.ae/blog/property-and-community-service-charges-in-dubai); [Pearlshire](https://www.pearlshire.com/blogs/dubai-service-charges-2026)
- Tilal Al Ghaf villas and townhouses: ~AED 3-5/sqft, paid via Mollak. — [Lida](https://withlida.com/areas/tilal-al-ghaf)
- Mollak / the DLD Service Charge Index is the authoritative per-building and per-community source; Driven publishes a guide to using it. — [Driven SCI guide](https://www.drivenproperties.com/dubai-real-estate-market-guide/service-charge-index); [Continental Club SCI](https://continentalclub.ae/dubai-service-charge-index/); [Mulki](https://mulki.ae/guides/service-charges-dubai)

### Inferences (illustrative annual cost = rate × built-up area)

| Home type | Assumed size | Rate (AED/sqft/yr) | Annual service charge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arabian Ranches 2 villa | 2,500 sqft | ~2.4 | ~AED 6k |
| Arabian Ranches / Dubai Hills / Tilal villa | 2,500-3,000 sqft | 3-5 | AED 7.5-15k |
| DAMAC Hills villa | 2,500 sqft | 3-10 | AED 7.5-25k (uncertain) |
| JGE villa | 3,500 sqft | 12-18 (if accurate) | AED 42-63k |

- **Buyers.** Service charges on a 3-bed apartment can cost as much per year as 1-1.5 percentage points of gross yield. On a villa in AR or Springs-type communities they are typically under 0.3%. This favours buying villas or townhouses over prime apartments for total cost.
- **Tenants.** The landlord pays service charges, but high-charge buildings tend to push asking rents up.

## Q4. Which communities are seen as underpriced or overpriced relative to peers or recent transactions?

### Takeaway
No institutional analyst list surfaced. The signals point to:
- **Better value / possibly underpriced:** DAMAC Hills (cheapest established golf community), Mudon, Al Furjan (metro access), and Arabian Ranches 1-2 relative to Dubai Hills.
- **Rich or stretched:** Arabian Ranches 3 and Dubai South rents, where new supply is pulling asking rents down; The Springs, where asking prices sit above recent sales; and Sobha Hartland, after +12.8% in a year.

### Cited Findings
- Arabian Ranches 3 had the steepest villa asking-rent fall (-18.9% YoY, to AED 300k), and Dubai South fell 12.45%. This is consistent with new handovers adding supply. — [Arabian Business](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/real-estate/dubai-rents-q3-2026); [mid-east.info](https://mid-east.info/q3-2025-vs-q3-2026-dubai-rental-prices-property-finder/)
- The Springs: median asking 3-bed ~AED 5.82M (Allsopp) against an actual Springs 10 3-bed townhouse sale at AED 4.4M (Jul 2026). Prices rose 9.8% YoY while rents stayed flat, and Cresco cautions that prices "may be turning". — [Allsopp](https://www.allsoppandallsopp.com/dubai/dubai-communities/the-springs-area-guide); [Lyukos](https://lyukos.com/areas/emirates-living/); [Cresco](https://crescorealestate.ae/area-guides/springs-meadows-lakes/)
- Sobha Hartland: ~AED 2,476/sqft in 2026, +12.8% vs 2025. — [VIBGYOR](https://vibgyorrealestate.com/best-villa-communities-in-dubai-2026/)
- DAMAC Hills, at ~AED 1,908/sqft, is "one of the lowest-priced established communities". — [Lyukos](https://lyukos.com/buy-villa-in-dubai/)
- Mudon suits families wanting larger properties "at a comparatively moderate price point". Al Furjan is flagged for rare metro access. Mirdif is flagged as budget on the rent side but not on the buy side. — [Swank best areas](https://swankdevelopment.com/best-areas-to-live-in-dubai/); [DSQ](https://www.dsqrealestate.ae/articles/best-areas-to-live-in-dubai-2026/)
- Al Furjan: Bayut listing averages (~4.25M for a 3-bed, ~4.73M for a 4-bed) sit well below a quoted DLD 4-bed median of ~6.5M; the source attributes the gap to cheaper listed stock. — [search summary, agency source](https://swankdevelopment.com/best-places-to-buy-a-villa-in-dubai/)
- Tilal Al Ghaf: AED/sqft estimates range from 1,552 to 2,446 depending on source. The DLD 12-month median of 2,446 is close to Dubai Hills, which suggests it trades at near-prime pricing. — [Throne](https://throneproperties.ae/dubai-property-prices/tilal-al-ghaf/); [VIBGYOR](https://vibgyorrealestate.com/tilal-al-ghaf-dubai-prices-master-plan-and-yields-2026/)
- Dubai Hills Estate apartments were the only tracked apartment area with no fall in asking rents in Q3 2026, a sign of resilient demand. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/property/dubai-property-rental-prices-fall-where-they-were-once-the-highest-1.500714361)
- Downtown 3-bed and other apartment asking rents fell most (-21.7%), as rental listings rose 55%. — [Arabian Business](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/real-estate/dubai-rents-q3-2026)

### Inferences
- **For a renter in late 2026:** negotiating power is highest in Downtown, Business Bay and Marina apartments, and in newer villa supply (AR3, Dubai South/Emaar South, DAMAC Lagoons). Established villa communities (Springs, Dubai Hills, Arabian Ranches 1) have eased less.
- **For a buyer:**
  - Arabian Ranches 1-2 (~1,430-1,680/sqft, low service charges ~AED 2.4-3.1/sqft) look good value against Dubai Hills (~2,400-2,900/sqft).
  - DAMAC Hills offers golf-community living for ~1,900/sqft.
  - The Springs/Meadows and Sobha Hartland look fully priced after strong 2025-26 gains.
  - Asking-vs-transaction gaps of 20%+ (e.g. Springs) suggest offering against DLD sold comps, not asking prices.
