# Renting or Buying a 3-4 Bed Home in Dubai as an Expat (2026): Process, Costs, Rules, Negotiation and Risks

Research date: 27 Nov 2026. Method note: web search plus attempted fetches. Several hosts (khaleejtimes.com, realestateclubdubai.com, angelindubai.com) were blocked in this session's network, so many findings rely on search-result summaries of those pages rather than full-page reads. Most fee/rate figures come from broker, agency and developer blogs; official DLD/CBUAE pages were not fetched in full. Verify all numbers with DLD, CBUAE and lenders before acting.

## Buying costs (DLD transfer fee, admin, agency, mortgage registration, trustee, valuation, bank fees, NOC) and 2025-2026 changes

### Takeaway
Budget roughly 7-8% of the price on top of the purchase for a mortgaged resale (4% DLD + ~2% agency + VAT + trustee + mortgage registration + bank/valuation fees), plus the down payment. No change to the 4% DLD fee was found for 2025-2026; sources disagree only on small admin/trustee figures and whether VAT is included.

### Cited Findings
- DLD transfer fee is 4% of the price; Dubai has no other purchase tax. Applies to ready, off-plan and secondary sales — [Engel & Völkers 2026 guide](https://www.engelvoelkers.com/ae/en/resources/cost-of-buying-property-in-dubai); [JRE Dubai](https://jre.ae/guides/dld-fees-and-dubai-property-buying-costs-explained)
- Trustee office (registration trustee) fee: AED 2,000 (<AED 500k) / AED 4,000 (>=AED 500k) + 5% VAT, i.e. AED 2,100 / AED 4,200 VAT-inclusive; sources differ on whether VAT is quoted — [DLD Fees 2026, Kingdom by Mag](https://kingdombymag.ae/dld-dubai-land-department-fees-explained-for-property-buyers-in-2025-26/); [Dubai Off-plan Investor](https://www.dubaioffplaninvestor.com/guides/dld-fees-buying-costs/)
- DLD admin / title deed fee: about AED 580 for ready property (AED 40 quoted for off-plan by one source); sources differ — [Real Estate Club Dubai, DLD fees 2026](https://realestateclubdubai.com/blog/legal/dld-service-fees-2026-trustee-office-noc-mortgage-registration-every-charge-explained); [Egsh](https://egsh.ae/insights/cost-of-buying-property-dubai)
- Mortgage registration (DLD): 0.25% of the loan amount + AED 290 — [Mortgease 2026](https://mortgease.ae/blog/cost-of-buying-property-dubai/); [withlida](https://withlida.com/insights/cost-of-buying-property-dubai)
- Bank arrangement/processing fee: typically 0.5%-1.0% of the loan (some banks waive/cap); valuation fee ~AED 2,500-3,500 — [Mortgease](https://mortgease.ae/blog/cost-of-buying-property-dubai/); [Real Estate Club Dubai hidden costs](https://realestateclubdubai.com/blog/buying-guide/dubai-real-estate-fees-complete-hidden-costs-breakdown)
- Agency commission on resales: customarily 2% + 5% VAT, paid by buyer; off-plan from developer usually no buyer commission — [Engel & Völkers](https://www.engelvoelkers.com/ae/en/resources/cost-of-buying-property-in-dubai)
- Total transaction costs typically 7-8% of price; off-plan lower (no agency fee, usually no trustee fee) — [Engel & Völkers](https://www.engelvoelkers.com/ae/en/resources/cost-of-buying-property-in-dubai); [Global Investments](https://www.globalinvestments.net/properties/guides/full-buying-costs-breakdown-dubai)
- Developer NOC (needed for resale transfer): about AED 500-5,000 depending on developer (e.g., Nakheel ~AED 500, Emaar ~AED 1,000, DAMAC ~AED 5,000), usually paid by seller, often + VAT. A 2011 Dubai directive told developers to charge AED 500, but current practice varies — [Driven Properties MOU guide](https://www.drivenproperties.com/blog/mou-dubai-real-estate); [Emirates 24/7, 2011](https://www.emirates247.com/property/dubai-developers-told-to-charge-dh500-for-noc-2011-06-09-1.401818); [Oliva NOC docs](https://joinoliva.com/en/learn/blog/required-documents-for-noc-application)
- The 4% DLD fee is "often paid by the buyer, but negotiable in some cases"; developers sometimes waive/absorb DLD fee on off-plan as an incentive — [Aigents Realty negotiation tips](https://aigentsrealty.com/blog/dubai-property-negotiation-tips-buyers-guide)
- Selling later costs ~2% more (agency), so round-trip friction is ~10% — [rent vs buy roundup, Real Estate Club Dubai](https://realestateclubdubai.com/blog/buying-guide/renting-vs-buying-property-in-dubai-2026-complete-10-year-cost-comparison)

### Inferences
- Worked example (inferred from the above fee rates, not from a source): AED 4,000,000 villa, 80% mortgage (AED 3.2M): DLD 4% = 160,000; agency 2% + VAT = 84,000; trustee ~4,200; title deed ~580; mortgage registration 0.25% x 3.2M + 290 = 8,290; bank fee 0.5-1% = 16,000-32,000; valuation ~3,000. Total ~AED 276k-292k (~7%), plus AED 800k down payment => roughly AED 1.08M cash needed.
- Cash buyers save mortgage registration, bank fee and valuation (~0.8-1.3% of price).

### Gaps
- Could not fetch the official DLD fee schedule to confirm exact 2026 admin/title deed fees and whether any fee changed in 2025-2026. No credible report of a change to the 4% rate was found.
- Conveyancing lawyer fees (optional in Dubai; often AED 5,000-15,000) — no reliable 2026 source found.

## Mortgages for expats (LTV, tenor, age, DBR, 2026 rates/EIBOR, pre-approval, non-residents)

### Takeaway
Resident expats: max 80% LTV on a first home <= AED 5M, 70% above; off-plan 50%; second homes lower (60%/65% per standard CBUAE rules). Max tenor 25 years; DBR cap 50%. Rates in late 2026 roughly 3.9%-4.2% fixed (1-5 yrs) vs variable 3M EIBOR (~4.3% Nov 2026) + ~1-2% margin, so fixed currently looks cheaper than variable.

### Cited Findings
- LTV, expat residents: up to 80% for first home under AED 5M, 70% above AED 5M; UAE nationals +5 pts; off-plan capped at 50% for all — [Datahabibi mortgage guide 2026](https://datahabibi.ae/blog/dubai-mortgage-guide); [House and Hedges 2026](https://houseandhedges.com/blog/dubai-mortgage-rules-2026-ltv-limits-rates-dbr-caps-what-buyers-need-to-know)
- Underlying regulation: CBUAE Regulations Regarding Mortgage Loans (2013, Notice 31/2013) — [CBUAE Rulebook](https://rulebook.centralbank.ae/node/2850); [Al Tamimi summary](https://www.tamimi.com/law-update/december-january-1/articles/the-new-uae-mortgage-regulations/)
- Max mortgage tenor: 25 years (official rulebook) — [CBUAE Rulebook](https://rulebook.centralbank.ae/node/2850)
- Age: the 2019 amendment removed the regulatory age-70 cap; lenders set age limits by policy. Market norm for salaried expats ~65 at final repayment, ~70 for self-employed (bank practice, not law) — [Pak Banker via PressReader, Oct 2019](https://www.pressreader.com/pakistan/the-pak-banker/20191015/281767040988020); [Real Estate Club Dubai over-60s, Jul 2026](https://realestateclubdubai.com/blog/buying-guide/dubai-mortgage-over-60s-retirees-2026-age-limits-pension-income)
- DBR cap: total monthly debt payments <= 50% of gross monthly income — [Binghatti expat guide](https://www.binghatti.com/en-uk/blog/mortgage-dubai-for-expats); [Oplus Realty](https://oplusrealty.com/?p=23499)
- Non-residents: typically 50%-60% LTV (bank policy) and rates ~0.5-1% higher than residents — [Mortgage Compare expat rates](https://mortgagecompare.ae/knowledge-hub/expat-mortgage-rates-uae.html); [Mortgage Compare 2026 eligibility](https://mortgagecompare.ae/knowledge-hub/mortgages-for-expats-in-the-uae-2026-eligibility-guide.html)
- EIBOR: conflicting snapshots — ~3.85% 3M EIBOR late June 2026; ~4.45% after a September 2026 hike (base rate 3.90%); ~4.28% 3M EIBOR on 6 Nov 2026 — [Mortgage Compare, Jul 2026](https://mortgagecompare.ae/knowledge-hub/best-mortgage-rates-uae.html); [SAT Real Estate](https://satrealestate.com/dubai-mortgage-rates-2026/); [Angel in Dubai, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.angelindubai.com/post/dubai-mortgage-home-loan-rates-compared)
- Fixed rates: from ~3.89% (1/2/3/5-yr fixed at one bank, Nov 2026); other source: from 3.99% (up to 3-yr) and 4.19% (5-yr) for residents — [Angel in Dubai, Nov 2026](https://www.angelindubai.com/post/dubai-mortgage-home-loan-rates-compared); [Mortgage Compare](https://mortgagecompare.ae/knowledge-hub/best-mortgage-rates-uae.html)
- Variable: EIBOR + ~1.00%-1.99% (one source) or 1.75%-3.25% (another) — [Datahabibi](https://datahabibi.ae/blog/dubai-mortgage-guide); [House and Hedges](https://houseandhedges.com/blog/dubai-mortgage-rules-2026-ltv-limits-rates-dbr-caps-what-buyers-need-to-know)
- Signing an MOU without confirmed financing is a common cause of MOU defaults (get pre-approval first) — [Driven Properties](https://www.drivenproperties.com/blog/mou-dubai-real-estate)

### Inferences
- With 3M EIBOR ~4.3% plus a 1-2% margin, variable loans price around 5.3%-6.3% vs fixed ~3.9%-4.2%; a 3-5 year fix looks better value as of Nov 2026, but check the post-fix "follow-on" rate (usually EIBOR + margin) and early-settlement fees (commonly 1% capped at AED 10,000 under CBUAE rules — not verified in this session).
- Upfront cash for an expat resident on an AED 3-5M home is ~20% + ~7% costs = ~27% of price; above AED 5M, ~30% + costs.
- Pre-approval (valid typically 60-90 days) should be in hand before Form F; using a broker (e.g., Holo, Mortgage Finder) is common and usually free to the buyer (paid by bank) — not verified via fetched source.

### Gaps
- Second-home LTV figures (commonly cited 65% <= AED 5M / 60% > AED 5M for expats) were not confirmed by a fetched source in this session.
- Could not fetch individual bank pages (Emirates NBD, ADCB, FAB, HSBC, Mashreq) — rates above are broker snapshots. Early settlement fee cap not verified.
- EIBOR snapshots conflict; the CBUAE publishes daily EIBOR — check the latest fix.

## Golden Visa via property

### Takeaway
A Dubai property (or combination) worth AED 2M+ qualifies for the 10-year Golden Visa; mortgaged and off-plan properties qualify. Since around February 2026 reports say the old requirement to have paid down a minimum amount (50%/AED 2M equity) on a mortgaged property was dropped, but sources still conflict — confirm with DLD/ICP.

### Cited Findings
- Property value of AED 2M+ supports a 10-year Golden Visa; mortgaged and off-plan can qualify — [Property Finder help](https://help.propertyfinder.ae/hc/en-us/articles/37816365548946-How-to-get-a-UAE-Golden-Visa-through-Dubai-property); [Modon 2026 guide](https://www.modon.com/real-estate/blog/how-to-get-the-uae-golden-visa-by-property-investment)
- Reports that the UAE scrapped the minimum payment/down-payment requirement for the property Golden Visa (early 2026); eligibility now tied to DLD valuation of AED 2M+, with a bank NOC for mortgaged property — [AGBI](https://www.agbi.com/news/uae-scraps-minimum-payment-for-property-golden-visa/); [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/uae-golden-visa-dubai-drops-minimum-down-payment-required-for-property-owners); [Metropolitan, 2026](https://metropolitan.realestate/media/news/uae-golden-visa-mortgage-down-payment-rule-change-2026/)
- Conflicting: a Wasl page still says mortgaged property is accepted only if at least AED 2M has been paid to the bank; Binayah says off-plan requires 50% equity — [Wasl](https://www.wasl.ae/en/node/618); [Binayah](https://www.binayah.com/dubai-golden-visa-property-2026-how-buying-a-home-can-get-you-10-year-residency/)
- Off-plan from approved developers counts on Oqood (off-plan registration) value per some sources; others say AED 2M must have been paid — [Property Finder blog](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/blog/golden-visa-properties/); [Estate Agent in Dubai news](https://estateagentindubai.squarespace.com/dubai-real-estate-news/way-cleared-for-dubais-off-plan-buyers-to-get-golden-visas-on-aed-2m-property-asset)
- Older context (Jan 2024): Dubai previously required 50% paid on mortgaged property — [The National, Jan 2024](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/money/2024/01/24/golden-visa-dubai-property/)

### Inferences
- A 3-4 bed villa/townhouse in most family communities will exceed AED 2M, so Tom likely qualifies on value alone if bought; the open question is documentation (bank NOC, DLD valuation) rather than equity.

## Renting rules (Ejari, Smart Rental Index, rent caps, notice, deposits, fees, DEWA, housing fee, eviction, 2025-2026 changes)

### Takeaway
Rent increases are capped by Decree 43/2013 bands referenced to the DLD Smart Rental Index (launched 2 Jan 2025, building-level star ratings), and require 90 days' written notice before expiry. Eviction for sale/own use needs 12 months' notarised/registered-post notice. Move-in costs typically add ~10-15% of annual rent on top (deposit 5%, agency ~5% + VAT, DEWA AED 4,000 villa deposit, Ejari), and 5% municipality housing fee is billed monthly via DEWA.

### Cited Findings
- Smart Rental Index replaced the old RERA index on 2 Jan 2025; buildings get 1-5 star ratings; permitted rent follows building rating rather than area averages — [Is This Rent Fair, 2026](https://www.isthisrentfair.com/rera-smart-rental-index-explained); [McCone Properties](https://www.mcconeproperties.com/guides/rera-rental-index)
- Cap bands (Decree 43/2013, unchanged by 2025 index): rent within 10% of index average: 0%; 11-20% below: up to 5%; 21-30% below: up to 10%; 31-40% below: up to 15%; >40% below: up to 20% — [Primadom 2025](https://www.primadom.ae/blogs/dubai-rera-rent-calculator-2025-rental-index); [RentInIt](https://rentinit.ae/insights/landlord-guides/dubai-renewal-rent-increase/). (Engel & Völkers lists slightly different bands with no 15% tier — [E&V](https://www.engelvoelkers.com/ae/en/resources/what-is-rera-rental-index-calculator-how-to-use-it))
- Landlord must give written notice of any change in terms (incl. rent increase) at least 90 days before expiry; increases only at renewal — [Splendor 2025](https://splendor.ae/blogs/notice-period-for-rent-increase-in-dubai-what-tenants-and-landlords-need-to-know-in-2025/)
- DLD rent calculator (website / Dubai REST app) applies the bands — [Splendor](https://splendor.ae/blogs/notice-period-for-rent-increase-in-dubai-what-tenants-and-landlords-need-to-know-in-2025/)
- Core law: Law No. 26 of 2007 as amended (Law 33/2008) remains in force in 2026 — [Egsh Dubai rental law](https://egsh.ae/insights/dubai-rental-law); [Legal 500](https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/united-arab-emirates/real-estate-and-construction/dubai-landlord-tenant-law-|-landlord-and-tenant-rights)
- Eviction at end of tenancy for sale, own/first-degree-relative use, or major renovation/demolition requires 12 months' notice served via notary public or registered post (Art. 25(2)); own-use requires proof of no other suitable property; disputes go to the Rental Disputes Centre — [Chambers](https://chambers.com/articles/landlord-eviction-notice-in-dubai-when-is-a-12-month-notice-valid); [Property Finder eviction](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/blog/eviction-notice/)
- Eviction notices can now be transferred to a buyer with the sale of the property — [Arabian Business](https://arabianbusiness.com/industries/real-estate/dubai-real-estate-eviction-notices-can-now-be-transferred-with-property-sale)
- Security deposit: ~5% of annual rent unfurnished, ~10% furnished; refundable — [Bayut hidden costs](https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/hidden-costs-renting-dubai/); [Property Finder](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/blog/look-out-for-the-6-hidden-costs-of-renting-in-dubai)
- Agency fee: ~5% of annual rent (+VAT), sometimes min AED 5,000; Dubizzle cites 2-5% — [Property Finder](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/guides/the-additional-costs-of-renting-a-property/); [Dubizzle](https://www.dubizzle.com/blog/property/hidden-costs-renting-dubai/)
- DEWA security deposit: AED 2,000 apartments / AED 4,000 villas, refundable; activation fee ~AED 130 — [Gulf News, 11 extra costs](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/housing/dubai-rental-costs-ejari-dewa-deposits-moving-fees-1.500296020); [Bayut](https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/rental-costs-dubai)
- Dubai Municipality housing fee: 5% of annual rent, paid by tenant in 12 monthly instalments via DEWA bill — [Bayut](https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/hidden-costs-renting-dubai/); [Khaleej Times renting guide](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/your-guide-to-renting-in-dubai)
- Ejari registration ~AED 120-235 depending on channel — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/housing/dubai-rental-costs-ejari-dewa-deposits-moving-fees-1.500296020)
- 2026 changes: Dubai Law No. 4 of 2026 on shared housing (reported effective ~26 Aug 2026; permits per shared unit, fines up to AED 1M; tenants barred from subletting) — [Stalk Dubai](https://stalkdubai.com/dubai-shared-housing-law-2026/); [Veersant](https://veersant.com/blog/apartment-sharing-dubai-shared-housing-law-2026/). Explanatory memorandum (8 Mar 2026) on Article 34: landlords may not cut services; Dubai Police can order restoration without prior court order — [Real Estate Club Dubai new laws 2026](https://realestateclubdubai.com/blog/renting/new-rental-laws-dubai-2026-tenants-landlords-need-to-know)
- A proposed 3-year rent freeze reported by Gulf News was a draft (old) and is not current law — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/property/dubai-landlords-try-to-get-higher-rents-on-new-tenancy-contracts-before-3-year-freeze-takes-effect-1.1624505586981)
- Monthly rent payment options exist but are at landlord discretion — [Real Estate Club Dubai](https://realestateclubdubai.com/blog/renting/new-rental-laws-dubai-2026-tenants-landlords-need-to-know)

### Inferences
- For an AED 300k/yr villa, upfront move-in cash (excluding rent cheques): deposit 15,000 + agency ~15,750 (5% + VAT) + DEWA 4,130 + Ejari ~200 + moving ≈ AED 35-40k; plus 5% housing fee (AED 15k/yr) spread over DEWA bills.
- The rent cap protects renewals only; first-year rent on a new lease is fully market-negotiated, so negotiate hardest at signing.

### Gaps
- Rent cap band wording varies by source; official DLD calculator not fetched.
- Rental Valuation Certificate / Rental Disputes Centre fees (~3.5% of annual rent for a case) not verified this session.
- Several 2026 law items come from a single blog; official Dubai legislation portal not fetched.

## Rent vs buy (yields, price-to-rent, breakeven, ownership costs)

### Takeaway
Villa gross yields ~5-6.5% (net ~3.5-4.8%), i.e. price-to-rent ~16-20x. With ~7-8% buy-in and ~2% exit costs, most analyses put breakeven at ~4-7 years; renting wins for stays under ~3 years. With prices softening in 2026, the case for buying now is weaker for short stays but buyers have more leverage.

### Cited Findings
- Average villa gross yield ~5.0%; net yields 1.5-2.5 pts lower after service charges, vacancy, maintenance — [Real Estate Club Dubai rental yield report 2025](https://realestateclubdubai.com/reports/dubai-rental-yield-report-2025/download)
- Arabian Ranches: 5.0%-6.4% gross, 3.5%-4.8% net; 3-beds give highest yields — [Oliva Arabian Ranches guide](https://joinoliva.com/en/learn/blog/arabian-ranches-dubai-villa-investment-guide)
- Global Property Guide publishes Dubai gross yields by type (not fetched) — [Global Property Guide](https://globalpropertyguide.com/middle-east/united-arab-emirates/rental-yields)
- Villa service charges: ~AED 4-7/sq ft in established villa communities; range AED 5-25/sq ft/yr by community; apartment towers add chiller (district cooling) charges, which apply even when vacant — [Oliva](https://joinoliva.com/en/learn/blog/arabian-ranches-dubai-villa-investment-guide); [Sands of Wealth](https://sandsofwealth.com/blogs/news/dubai-rental-yields-villa)
- Breakeven: ~4-6 years and ~5-7 years (different guides); rent if staying <3 years, buy if >7 years; buying ~7.5-8% upfront, ~2% to sell — [Real Estate Club Dubai 10-yr comparison](https://realestateclubdubai.com/blog/buying-guide/renting-vs-buying-property-in-dubai-2026-complete-10-year-cost-comparison); [PSI breakeven](https://www.psinv.net/de/blog/renting-vs-buying-the-break-even-point-in-the-uae-market); [The National, Jul 2026](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/money/2026/07/11/uae-property-is-it-still-worth-buying-a-house-in-dubai-after-more-than-15-years-of-renting/)
- Market: H1 2026 residential softened for first time since 2020 — volumes -13.9% YoY, values -15.7% (one report) — [Q1 2026 residential report (PDF)](https://a.storyblok.com/f/209096/x/bab0884ff3/dubai-real-estate-q1-2026-residential-report.pdf); The National (May 2026) said UAE property shifted toward a buyers' market for the first time in years — [The National](https://thenationalnews.com/business/2026/05/08/uae-property-shifts-towards-buyers-market-for-first-time-in-years/)
- Fitch (May 2025) forecast up to ~15% price decline over 2025-26 on supply; June 2026 Fortune report says Fitch now sees a larger correction amid weaker activity; ~120,000 handovers scheduled in 2026 — [The National, May 2025](https://thenationalnews.com/business/2025/05/29/dubai-property-prices-expected-to-fall-by-15-on-supply-glut-this-year-fitch-says); [Fortune, Jun 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/06/01/property-prices-down-dubai/)
- Counterview: some analysts say oversupply is segment-specific (apartments/off-plan) and deep correction risk is overstated — [Khaleej Times oversupply 2026](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/property/will-dubai-housing-market-see-an-oversupply-in-2026)

### Inferences
- Rough model (inferred): AED 4M villa, 5% gross yield = AED 200k rent. Owner with 80% loan at ~4% pays ~AED 128k interest yr1 + service charges/maintenance ~AED 20-40k, plus opportunity cost on ~AED 1.08M cash. Unrecoverable ownership costs are similar to rent; the ~10% round-trip friction (~AED 400k) needs ~4-6 years of rent saving/appreciation to recover — consistent with the cited 4-7 year range, and longer if prices fall further.
- Villas (family supply) are less exposed to the 2026 apartment/off-plan handover glut than towers, but price momentum has turned.

### Gaps
- No authoritative 2026 dataset of 3-4 bed villa price-to-rent by community found (only blog estimates; Engel & Völkers figures conflicting).

## Negotiation tactics in the current market

### Takeaway
2026 is a softer, more buyer-friendly market: use DLD/REST transaction data for comps, target vacant and motivated sellers (price-cut listings, handover-squeezed investors), offer certainty (pre-approval, fast close), and for rentals trade fewer cheques/longer tenure for lower rent. Negotiate terms (service charge waivers, DLD fee, payment plans) as well as price.

### Cited Findings
- Pull comparable sales from the DLD REST app for the same layout in the same building/community over the last 3-6 months before offering — [Aigents Realty](https://aigentsrealty.com/blog/dubai-property-negotiation-tips-buyers-guide); DLD data is strong evidence but not a full assessment — [DP Properties](https://dp.ae/blog/post/198/what-dld-data-reveals-about-dubais-property-market/)
- Negotiating payment plan length or service-charge waivers can be worth more than a small headline cut; developers may offer discounts, flexible plans or DLD-fee waivers on off-plan — [Aigents Realty](https://aigentsrealty.com/blog/dubai-property-negotiation-tips-buyers-guide)
- Sellers had cut listed prices by a combined AED 2.36bn across 3,292 properties by end-May 2026 — [Fortune, Jun 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/06/01/property-prices-down-dubai/)
- Buyers find "more choice, better deals" — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/property/dubai-property-buyers-find-more-choice-better-deals-1.500737222); market "holds firm as buyers seek value, not distress" — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/property/dubai-property-market-holds-firm-as-buyers-seek-value-not-distress-opportunities)
- Cash-buyer discount claims of 3-5.5% vs mortgage offers exist but are unverified marketing — [Aigents Realty](https://aigentsrealty.com/blog/dubai-property-negotiation-tips-buyers-guide)
- Form F (DLD's standard MOU) normally requires a 10% deposit (security cheque held by the agency/trustee); financing failure is a common default cause — [Driven Properties](https://www.drivenproperties.com/blog/mou-dubai-real-estate); [Engel & Völkers MOU](https://www.engelvoelkers.com/ae/en/resources/mou-agreement-dubai-real-estate)
- Holo's guide on negotiating price as a buyer — [Holo](https://www.useholo.com/en/blog/negotiating-the-property-price-when-buying-a-house-in-dubai)

### Inferences
- Rentals: offering 1-2 cheques instead of 4-12 is the classic lever for a discount (widely reported in agency guides, not verified this session); summer (Jun-Aug) is typically quieter, and the 2026 handover wave adds vacant supply, both favouring tenants.
- Vacant properties avoid the "tenanted sale" problem (buyer inheriting a lease and needing 12-month notice to occupy) — a tenanted villa should be priced lower if Tom wants to live in it.
- Add Form F special conditions: subject to mortgage approval/valuation, seller-paid NOC, handover condition, and a clear completion date; get a lawyer to review the deposit forfeiture clause.

## Legal checks, scams, off-plan risk, forms

### Takeaway
Verify every agent (BRN/ORN via Dubai REST e-card), every listing (Trakheesi permit + Madmoun QR), and the landlord/seller identity against the title deed before paying anything. Insist on Ejari for rentals and pay deposits only into traceable accounts. For off-plan, payments must go into the project's DLD escrow account (Law 8/2007), but escrow does not prevent delays.
